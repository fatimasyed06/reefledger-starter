What is coral bleaching?

Stressed coral can lose the algae that provide much of its color and energy. Bleaching does not necessarily mean the coral has died.

What counts as damage?

Reported physical damage may involve broken colonies, discarded equipment, or storm impacts. An image alone may not establish a cause.

What does the history tell us?

A timeline records when different people submitted claims. It is not automatically a scientific measurement of reef recovery or restoration.

ReefLedger is a citizen-reporting prototype, not a verified scientific dataset. Coordinates and dates are self-reported/submission metadata; grouped reports are geographically close, not necessarily the same reef site. Downloaded CSVs contain publicly submitted claims. Photos can be changed at third-party URLs unless verified against a nonzero on-chain SHA-256 fingerprint.
